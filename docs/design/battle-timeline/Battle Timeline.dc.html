Why the ×2 states merge
Rock Slide then Super Power, or the reverse, both shielded: each shield blocks all but 1 damage, Melmetal spends 85 energy either way and ends at Attack and Defense −1. Lanturn's Spark rounds to 3 damage at both Defense stages, so HP matches too. Same state, same hash, one node.
Why the others don't
When Rock Slide lands after Super Power, it hits at Attack −1 for 24 instead of 30. Order changes the damage, so Lanturn's HP and the hash differ.
