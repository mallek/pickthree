# Claude Design prompt: Your meta (battle log)

Paste everything below the line into the existing PickThree project in Claude Design so it
inherits the Nocturne token set, the type colors, the Pokemon token, and the phone frame.
Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-16-your-meta-design.md.

---

Extend the PickThree prototype with a battle log feature called Your meta. Same 390 x 844
phone frame, same tokens, dark default and light, Inter only. Keep every rule from the
existing screens: plain Pokemon names, first-use explanations of PvP terms, labels before
numbers, no em dashes, ASCII only in copy. Pokemon are shown with the existing Pokemon token
(sprite inside the type-colored circle, initial fallback). No other artwork.

## Navigation changes (apply to every existing screen)

1. The bottom tab bar becomes four tabs: Teams, Counters, Collection, Your meta. Remove the
   Filters tab. Pick an icon for Your meta that reads as "log" or "record" at 24 px.
2. Every screen header gets a settings cog at the right edge. It opens the existing sheet,
   now titled "Settings" instead of "Filters and settings".
3. On Teams, next to the existing filters chip, add a chip "Your log: 42 battles" that links to
   Your meta. Show a second state "Your log: 13 of 15".

## Screen: Your meta

Header: "Your meta", league switcher (existing component, Great League selected), cog.

Top to bottom:

1. Status line, one sentence, muted text, in one of three states. Design all three:
   - "Weighting Teams and Counters by 42 battles this season."
   - "13 of 15 battles until your log weights Teams and Counters." with a thin progress bar.
   - "Your log is switched off in Settings."
   Below the first state, a one-time explainer card that can be dismissed: "Once you log 15
   battles, Teams and Counters weigh opponents by how often you actually face them. Your log
   never leaves this phone."
2. Current set card. Title "Set 7", started "Today 6:40 pm". Your team as three Pokemon tokens
   with names (Tinkaton, Azumarill, Galarian Corsola). Five slots in a row: the first three
   filled (W, L, tanked), two empty. A filled slot shows the result letter in a small badge and
   up to three tiny opponent tokens under it. The tanked slot is greyed with the word "tanked".
   Two buttons: primary "Log a battle", ghost "End set". Also design the empty state of this
   card: no open set, one primary button "New set".
3. Section "Most faced this season", with a small cog popover on the section header holding two
   sort options: "Most faced" and "Worst record". Rows: token, name, "faced 14" and the record
   "9-5" as W-L, with a subtle bar behind the row proportional to faced. Rows to show:
   Tinkaton 14 (9-5), Azumarill 11 (4-7), Shadow Dragonite 9 (2-7), Galarian Corsola 8 (6-2),
   Clodsire 7 (5-2), Feraligatr 6 (3-3). Shadow Dragonite gets a small tag "not in PvPoke's
   list" so the player learns why it matters that it was logged. Tapping a row goes to Counters
   for that Pokemon (no new screen needed; just show the tap affordance).
4. Section "Your teams this season". Rows: three tokens, "12 battles", record "8-4". Two rows.
   Tapping opens Build with that team.
5. Section "Earlier seasons", collapsed by default, chevron. Expanded state shows one sub-header
   per season ("Forever Forward, 31 battles") with the same two lists in a compact form.
6. Warning state, design once as a variant of the status line: "The season list may be out of
   date." with a ghost button "Start fresh". Warn color tint, not red.

## Screen: New set

Sheet or full screen, your call, titled "Pick your team". Three sections in order:
- "Recent teams" from the log: rows of three tokens with names, most recent first (two rows).
- "From pick3": teams from the current recommendation (three rows, each with its Strong/Solid
  label as on Teams).
- "Pick three": three empty slots plus a search field. Typing shows matches from the
  collection first (with the specimen's CP), then "Other Pokemon" for species not owned.
Primary button "Start set" enabled once three slots are filled.

## Screen: Log a battle

The screen must work between two ranked battles with one thumb. Title "Log a battle",
subtitle "Set 7, battle 4 of 5". Close (X) at top left.

1. Three opponent slots in a row, large, empty by default with a dotted outline and "1", "2",
   "3". A filled slot shows the token and name under it, tap to clear. Design the state with
   two filled and one empty; partial is normal, not an error.
2. "Recent" label and a wrapping row of about 20 small Pokemon tokens with short names, most
   recently faced first. Tapping one fills the next empty slot. Include Shadow Dragonite with
   the shadow marker the app already uses.
3. Search field "Search any Pokemon" with a results list of tokens and names (show three
   results for "dra": Dragonite, Shadow Dragonite, Dragonair).
4. Result row pinned at the bottom above the safe area: three equal buttons "Win", "Loss",
   "Tanked". Win and Loss are primary weight; Tanked is ghost with a one-line note under the
   row on first use: "Tanked means they quit or threw. It stays in the set but counts for
   nothing." Tapping any of the three saves immediately and returns to Your meta.
5. After the fifth battle, a small confirmation state: "Set 7 done, 3-2." with two buttons:
   primary "New set, same team", ghost "Back to Your meta".

## Settings sheet additions

In the existing sheet, add a section "Your meta" after League:
- Toggle "Use your log" with sub-label "Weights Teams, Counters and Build by what you actually
  face. Kicks in at 15 battles." On by default.
- Row "Start fresh in Great League" with sub-label "Moves battles before now to Earlier
  seasons. Nothing is deleted." Tapping shows a small confirm with "Start fresh" and "Cancel".
- Two ghost buttons side by side: "Export log", "Import log". Sub-label: "A file on this phone.
  Your log never leaves it unless you share the file."

## Assumptions line on Team detail

The existing assumptions block on Team detail gains one line. Show both variants:
"Weighted by your log: 42 battles this season, 3 opponents outside PvPoke's list simulated."
and "PvPoke weights only (13 of 15 battles logged)."

## Deliverables

Add the new screens to the prototype file with the same screen-label convention (Your meta,
New set, Log a battle) and update Teams, Team detail, and the Settings sheet in place. Keep
the tokens file unchanged unless a new semantic token is unavoidable; if one is, name it and
say why.
