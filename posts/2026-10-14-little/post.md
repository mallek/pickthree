**Counters to Wynaut, Bronzor and Hoothoot (cheap)**

Wynaut, Bronzor and Hoothoot are what most people are running, and Wynaut and Bronzor are the two most expensive Pokémon on the Top list (about 515k and 334k stardust from a level 20 start). These teams are picked to beat those three, with every Pokémon under 75k stardust and 75 candy. The number is pick3's team sim against Wynaut, Bronzor and both Hoothoots only, not the whole meta. For comparison, the #1 Top team (Wynaut + Impidimp + Ledyba) scores 83.9 on the same test and costs about 660k stardust.

1. [Shadow Nosepass, Impidimp, Onix](https://pick3.gg/#/t/little/nosepass_shadow.ROCK_THROW.ROCK_SLIDE.ROCK_BLAST+impidimp.SUCKER_PUNCH.FOUL_PLAY.PLAY_ROUGH+onix.ROCK_THROW.ROCK_SLIDE.SAND_TOMB) \- 85.7, about 209k stardust
   * Impidimp beats Wynaut at every shield count and Bronzor with 1 or 2 shields. Onix and Shadow Nosepass beat both Hoothoots at every shield count.
2. [Litwick, Impidimp, Shadow Nosepass](https://pick3.gg/#/t/little/litwick.EMBER.MYSTICAL_FIRE.HEAT_WAVE+impidimp.SUCKER_PUNCH.FOUL_PLAY.PLAY_ROUGH+nosepass_shadow.ROCK_THROW.ROCK_SLIDE.ROCK_BLAST) \- 84.9, about 209k stardust
   * The best of these against the whole meta, not just the three.
3. [Nosepass, Impidimp, Riolu](https://pick3.gg/#/t/little/nosepass.ROCK_THROW.ROCK_SLIDE.ROCK_BLAST+impidimp.SUCKER_PUNCH.FOUL_PLAY.PLAY_ROUGH+riolu.COUNTER.CROSS_CHOP.BLAZE_KICK) \- 84.4, about 137k stardust
   * The cheapest. Riolu is a low cost third slot that does not beat the three on its own, so Impidimp and Nosepass do the work.

Impidimp is in all three. It is the only cheap Pokémon that beats Wynaut and Bronzor, and the Rock types handle Hoothoot.

Budget Cores - Low Stardust only lists Pokémon under 75k stardust and 75 candy each, counted from a level 20 start, with no Elite TM moves. Little Cup has no evolutions, so that is the full cost.

How the number works: pick3 simulates each team against the Little Cup meta, weighted by what players face (PvPoke meta combined with trainer reported matches on pick3). It is a projection, not a measured win rate.
